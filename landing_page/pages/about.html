ABOUT LIQUIDNAT

For the people
behind the packets.

Broadband networks connect everyday life. The software behind them should give operators a clearer understanding of the service they deliver.

OUR POINT OF VIEW

Complex networks.
Human operations.

LiquidNAT is built around a simple idea: the edge, subscriber identity and management experience belong in the same conversation. Separate components should still contribute to a connected understanding.

Our product family — LiquidNAT Gateway, LiquidNAT RADIUS and LiquidNAT Manager — connects those responsibilities through a software-first architecture for ISP-operated infrastructure.

Recently acquired by LiquidWebSpace LTD, LiquidNAT is entering a new chapter. Major changes ahead build on that direction, with a focus on a connected product experience and a clearer relationship with network operators.
