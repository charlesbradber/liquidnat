A GATEWAY WITH CONTEXT

From access circuit to internet.

01 /

Broadband network gateway

Plan PPPoE or IPoE termination around subscriber identity, circuit information, address assignment and service policy. Include the handoff, VLAN/QinQ and MTU in your design.

02 /

Carrier-grade NAT

Connect private subscriber addressing to public IPv4 resources. Consider port allocation, TCP/UDP/ICMP behaviour, exhaustion and mapping traceability together.

03 /

Dual-stack services

Treat IPv4 and IPv6 as one service. Include prefix delegation, neighbour discovery, path MTU and subscriber policy in both directions.

04 /

Routing integration

Align forwarding intent with BGP/OSPF control through FRRouting integration. Review route policy, next-hop behaviour and traffic symmetry.

05 /

Service enforcement

Define upload/download policy, aggregate limits, ACLs and subscriber resource limits as explicit, reviewable service intent.

06 /

Recovery planning

Separate configuration recovery, address ownership, NAT flow continuity and subscriber session continuity. Set expectations for each failure domain.

ADDRESS ECONOMICS

IPv4 is valuable.
So is your time.

A CGNAT decision goes beyond conserving addresses. You also need an answer when a pool fills, a subscriber consumes too many ports or an attribution query arrives.

Understand allocation

Compare public address use, subscriber port demand, pool separation and allocation modes for your traffic mix.

Think in tuples and time

Traceability needs the external address, port, protocol and timestamp, connected to allocation and subscriber history.

Plan for pressure

Separate address exhaustion, port exhaustion, state-table pressure and CPU limits in the capacity review.
