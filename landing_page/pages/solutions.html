MIGRATION, WITH INTENT

Move in steps you can explain.

  1. Discover

    Record protocols, handoffs, subscriber profiles, RADIUS attributes, pools, routes and commercial constraints.

  2. Map

    Translate the service model. Identify unsupported settings and dependencies before changing a circuit.

  3. Validate

    Exercise representative subscriber journeys. Compare traffic, policy, accounting and recovery outcomes.

  4. Transition

    Agree a maintenance window, a small cohort, success criteria and a tested return path.
